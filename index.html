Cluo Duo

Free online Codenames Duet alternative: a co-operative word game you play with an AI partner.

New game

Who gives the first clue?
How to play
  • You and your partner share 25 words. Each of you has a secret key: 9 of the words are your agents (green), 3 are assassins, and the rest are bystanders. Your keys overlap but aren't the same, so there are 15 agents to find between you.
  • Take turns giving a one-word clue and a number that point to agents on your own key. Your partner guesses words one at a time.
  • When you guess, your partner's key decides: an agent and you can keep going; a bystander ends the turn; an assassin loses the game.
  • Clues can't be a word on the board or contain one (or be contained in one).
  • Find all 15 agents within 9 turns to win.
